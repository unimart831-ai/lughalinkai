LughaLink AI: Machine Translation of Kenyan Public Service Announcements

Few-shot NLLB · English → Kiswahili · Kikuyu · Ekegusii (guz_Latn extension) · DSA 4020A NLP

Kenya · Public information EN → Kiswahili (pivot) EN → Kikuyu (NLLB-native) EN → Ekegusii (vocab-extend) NLLB-200 · Navon A100 FastAPI demo

Introduction

Problem

English-first PSAs leave Kiswahili and indigenous speakers with weaker access to health, election, and safety notices.

Objective

Transferable MT for English PSAs → Kiswahili, Kikuyu & Ekegusii on a curated Kenyan corpus.

Approach

PSA filter → NLLB silver (SW/Kik) · template silver (Ekegusii) → fine-tune / zero-shot → browser UI.

Impact

Open digital public good with honest silver/gold and real/synthetic labeling.

Languages & guz_Latn

  • NLLB codes: eng_Latn → swh_Latn · kik_Latn
  • Ekegusii not in stock NLLB-200 / FLORES
  • We add token guz_Latn (Ekegusii + Latin), resize embeddings, init from kik_Latn, force as decoder BOS
  • Zero-shot therefore looks Kikuyu-like until few-shot NLLB finishes
