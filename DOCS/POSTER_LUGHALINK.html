LughaLink AI: Machine Translation of Kenyan Public Service Announcements

Few-shot NLLB transfer · English → Kiswahili & Kikuyu · DSA 4020A Natural Language Processing

Kenya · Public information EN → Kiswahili (pivot) EN → Kikuyu (indigenous) NLLB-200 · Navon A100 Hugging Face deploy

Introduction

Problem

English-first PSAs leave Kiswahili and indigenous-language speakers with weaker access to health, election, and safety notices.

Objective

Build a transferable MT system for English PSAs into Kiswahili and Kikuyu on a curated Kenyan corpus.

Approach

Framework-filtered PSA data → NLLB silver seeding → fine-tune → evaluate → Hub + browser UI.

Impact

Open, reproducible digital public good with honest silver/gold labeling.

Data

  • Sources: IEBC, MoH, gov/NGO sites via registry scrapers
  • PSA Framework filter (action / advisory tone)
  • 5,000 English PSAs · 1,615 real · 3,385 labeled synthetic
  • 8,409 sentences · PSA-only (no OPUS in train)
  • Silver seed: 10,400 pairs → 9,077 after auto-QC
