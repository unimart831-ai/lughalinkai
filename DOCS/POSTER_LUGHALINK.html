LughaLink AI: Machine Translation of Kenyan Public Service Announcements

Few-shot NLLB transfer · English → Kiswahili & Kikuyu · DSA 4020A Natural Language Processing

Kenya · Public information EN → Kiswahili (pivot) EN → Kikuyu (indigenous) NLLB-200 · Navon A100 Hugging Face deploy

Introduction

Problem

English-first PSAs leave Kiswahili and indigenous speakers with weaker access to health, election, and safety notices.

Objective

Transferable MT for English PSAs → Kiswahili & Kikuyu on a curated Kenyan corpus.

Approach

Framework-filtered PSAs → NLLB silver seed → fine-tune → eval → Hub + browser UI.

Impact

Open digital public good with honest silver/gold and real/synthetic labels.

Data

  • IEBC, MoH, gov/NGO scrapers · PSA Framework gate
  • 5,000 EN PSAs · 1,615 real · 3,385 synthetic
  • 8,409 sentences · PSA-only (no OPUS in train)
  • Silver: 10,400 → 9,077 after auto-QC

Prior work & method

  • NLLB-200: swh_Latn · kik_Latn; generic MT weak on PSA register
  • Kikuyu chosen over unsupported / weak indigenous codes
  • Normalize · glossary · stratified splits · length/lang QC
  • Serve Hub weights via FastAPI (no client model download)
